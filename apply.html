Be honest. We've all got things we'd miss and things we'd secretly be relieved to put down for a while.

Step 6 · Your moment

It doesn't have to be dramatic. Sometimes "I just need to slow down" is the real answer.

Step 7 · What you bring

This week is built with you, not just for you. What's something you could bring to the group?

A few sparks, in case it helps: maybe you like being behind a camera — filming or photography for our little media team. Maybe there's something from where you're from — a dish, a song, a game or tradition — you'd share on our intercultural evening. Or something you'd enjoy guiding the group through: a short workshop, a meditation, a morning stretch, a sport or game you love. And if it's none of those, it might simply be the calm or the energy you bring to a room. It doesn't have to be big or polished — just yours.

Step 8 · A bit about you

Do you sometimes face challenges in life?

In this project, we want to give a chance to people who may have fewer opportunities. So if you've had any kind of difficulty, please feel free to tell us — it can actually help you get selected.

Some examples:

  • Mental health problems or learning difficulties
  • Not having much money or support
  • Feeling left out or being treated unfairly
  • Living far away or in a small town
  • Coming from a migrant or minority background

You don't have to write a lot — a few words is enough. What you share stays private (only the coordinator from the sending NGO and the receiving NGO will read it).
